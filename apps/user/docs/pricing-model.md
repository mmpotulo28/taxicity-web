# TaxiCity App Pricing & Revenue Model

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the pricing strategy for the TaxiCity platform, focusing on a sustainable transaction-based revenue model. The core mechanism is a percentage-based **Service Fee** deducted from every passenger fare processed or booked through the app.

## The Pricing Model

### Core Service Fee

We propose a **5% Platform Fee** on the total fare for each passenger transaction completed via the TaxiCity app. This fee covers:

- App maintenance and hosting.
- Payment processing costs (if digital).
- Marketing and passenger acquisition.
- Driver support services.

**Formula:**
$$ \text{Platform Revenue} = \text{Fare Price} \times 0.05 $$

### Optimization Strategy

To ensure fairness and maximize adoption among drivers while maintaining profitability, the pricing model includes the following optimizations:

1. **Dynamic Capping (Long Distance Protection):**
   For trips where the fare exceeds R200, the fee is capped at a maximum of R10.00. This encourages drivers to accept long-distance trips through the app without feeling penalized by high absolute fees.

2. **Volume Incentives (Driver Loyalty):**
   Drivers completing more than 100 app-mediated trips per month qualify for a reduced fee tier of **3.5%** for the remainder of that month. This gamifies the system and encourages drivers to migrate their "street pick-up" passengers to the app.

3. **Digital vs. Cash Handling:**
    - **Digital Payments (In-App Wallet/Card):** Fee is deducted automatically at source.
    - **Cash Fares (Booked via App):** Fee is accrued as a debt on the driver's profile, payable weekly/monthly or deducted from their future digital earnings.

## Revenue Projections

### Key Assumptions for Estimation

To generate realistic projections, we use current operational data from similar markets:

- **Average Fare:** R35.00 (Blended average of short R20 and long R65 trips).
- **Active Trips per Taxi:** 8 trips per day.
- **App Adoption Rate:** Estimate of **10 passengers per trip** booking via the app (approx. 66% of a 15-seater capacity).
- **Operating Days:** 26 days per month (6 days/week).

**Unit Economics (Per Taxi, Per Day):**

- App Gross Merchandise Value (GMV): $R35.00 \times 10 \text{ passengers} \times 8 \text{ trips} = R2,800.00$
- Daily Revenue (at 5%): $R2,800.00 \times 0.05 = R140.00$

## Conclusion

With a 5% fee model, TaxiCity can generate significant revenue at scale. Even with a modest adoption of 500 taxis (a fraction of the Gauteng market), the platform generates over **R1.8 Million monthly**. Scaling to 5,000 taxis positions the company as a major revenue generator with nearly **R218 Million annually**.

The low percentage (5%) ensures the barrier to entry for drivers is minimal compared to ride-hailing services (which often charge 20-25%), making this model highly competitive for the shared-vehicle minibus taxi industry.
